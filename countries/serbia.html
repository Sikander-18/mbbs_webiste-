07 — Student Life

Life in Serbia

climate

Continental climate — warm summers (25–30°C), cold winters (−5°C). Pleasant spring and autumn.

food

Serbian food is meat-heavy but affordable. Indian restaurants available in Belgrade. Self-cooking is common.

safety

Serbia is a safe European country with low crime rates. Belgrade is a modern, vibrant city.

transport

Good bus and rail network. Belgrade has excellent public transport. Europe easily accessible by train.

currency

Serbian Dinar (RSD). Monthly living expenses ~€250–€350 (accommodation + food).

08 — FMGE / NExT

FMGE/NExT Preparation from Serbia

Serbia universities are not primarily MBBS programs — they offer bachelor's and master's degrees in various fields through Alfa BK University, and medicine through University of Kragujevac. Students targeting FMGE/Indian medical practice should consult our doctors about eligibility.

10 — FAQs

FAQ — MBBS in Serbia

What programs are available in Serbia?
Bachelor's and Master's programs at Alfa BK University (various fields), and Medicine at University of Kragujevac. Both are in Belgrade/Kragujevac — accessible European cities.
What is the total cost of studying in Serbia?
Bachelor's (girls): ~€1,000/year tuition. Bachelor's (boys): ~€1,950/year. Masters: €1,200–€2,100/year. Plus ~€150/month accommodation and €150/month food. One-time company charge: €1,000.
Can I work while studying in Serbia?
Yes! Stellar Science Hub & Educonsultancy helps students find part-time jobs while studying, and assists with placement in UAE and Germany after graduation.
Is the degree from Serbia recognised internationally?
Yes. Serbian university degrees are recognised across Europe. Stellar Science Hub handles 12th marksheet equivalence and recognition as part of the package.
How long is the visa processing?
Typically 2–4 weeks. Stellar Science Hub & Educonsultancy handles all visa documentation as part of the €500 visa processing service.
