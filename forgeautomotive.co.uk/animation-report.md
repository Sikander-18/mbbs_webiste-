# Forge Automotive — Full Site, Motion and Animation Audit

Reference: https://forgeautomotive.co.uk/  
Audit date: 8 September 2026  
Scope: all same-origin routes discovered through navigation, footer links, index pages and detail-page links. External social links, media hosts, telephone links and malformed email-as-URL links are excluded.

## 1. Executive summary

Forge Automotive is positioned as a premium automotive atelier rather than an ordinary modification shop. The visual language is cinematic: dark backgrounds, large editorial typography, full-bleed vehicle imagery, restrained copy, a sticky header and scroll-led scene changes. Motion is used to reveal the brand idea of identity, insight and cohesion, not only to decorate cards.

The conversion story is:

1. Establish a premium point of view.
2. Explain a considered process.
3. Present services.
4. Prove quality through completed builds and stock.
5. Move the visitor toward a consultation.

For an MBBS consultancy, the transferable lesson is the progression and clarity, not the automotive styling. The visitor should feel guided from aspiration to profile assessment, shortlist, application and counselling.

## 3. Information architecture and conversion

Primary journey:

Home → Approach → Services → Previous Builds → Stock or Contact

Persistent conversion elements:

- Start Your Project appears after the approach and service sections.
- Header exposes Home, Builds, Stock and Contact.
- Footer repeats core pages and legal pages.
- Header includes a call/contact affordance.
- Long pages include Back to Top.
- Contact uses a structured enquiry form.
- Stock detail has a purchase-oriented form.

### MBBS equivalent

Home → Why study medicine abroad → How we guide you → Countries and universities → Student proof → Eligibility and cost → Book counselling

The MBBS site should answer:

1. Can I trust the consultancy?
2. Can it help my academic and financial profile?
3. What is the next safe, clear action?

## 4. Homepage audit

### Entry gate

The initial experience uses a full-screen black layer with the Forge mark, a statement and an Enter action. Analytics/cookie messaging is associated with the entrance.

- Visual impact: 8/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 2/10.

This creates exclusivity but delays useful information. Do not use a forced entry gate for MBBS. Use a fast logo fade and show eligibility, fees and counselling access immediately.

### Sticky header

The header stays available with logo, menu and call/contact affordance.

- Visual impact: 7/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 9/10.

Adopt this pattern with Logo, Countries, Services, Student Stories, Fees/FAQ and Book Free Counselling. Keep it readable over images and simple on mobile.

### Hero

Observed message:

- We don’t modify vehicles.
- We build them for you.
- For Those Who Refuse Ordinary.
- A luxury automotive atelier for bespoke styling, performance and craftsmanship.

Motion uses full-screen media, large type and a transition into the next section rather than ending at a static banner.

- Visual impact: 9/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 8/10.

Recommended MBBS message:

“Your MBBS journey, planned around your score, budget and future.”

Use Check My Eligibility and Book Free Counselling. Use slow campus/student imagery, not fast cinematic effects.

### Our Approach To Every Build

The central story explains intentional decisions and introduces:

1. Identity.
2. Insight.
3. Cohesion.

Large visual panels and numbered states create a scroll-linked process. Repeated CTA placement keeps conversion available.

- Visual impact: 8/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 10/10.

Translate to:

1. Profile — NEET status, marks, budget, preferred country and future plan.
2. Shortlist — verified universities, eligibility, fees and realistic options.
3. Admission — documents, applications, visa and arrival support.

Animate the active stage, but keep all three stages selectable, text-readable and keyboard-accessible.

### Brand strip

Forge uses many automotive brand logos to communicate breadth.

- Visual impact: 6/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 8/10.

Use accurately labelled country coverage, official recognition/partner badges and support metrics. Avoid an unexplained moving logo strip.

### Statement transition

The line “A vehicle should say something before it moves. Every line, material, and finish is considered.” gives the site an emotional thesis.

- Visual impact: 9/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 5/10.

Use one statement such as “A medical degree is more than a seat. It is a decision about the life you want to build.” Follow it immediately with facts.

### Services

Forge presents Bodystyling, Interior, Wheels, Lighting, Exhaust and Protection. Each service includes concise copy, image treatment and CTA.

- Visual impact: 7/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 9/10.

Use University Selection, Eligibility/NEET Guidance, Application Support, Document Verification, Visa/Travel Support and Accommodation/Arrival Support. Never make critical text hover-only.

### Ordinary Ends Here

A sequence of vehicle images and Ends Here forms a visual climax.

- Visual impact: 9/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 4/10.

An MBBS version could say “Confusion ends here,” but it must be followed by transparent fees, verified choices, process steps and a CTA.

### Portfolio and stock close

The homepage closes with Previous Builds, Available Stock, a final brand CTA and Back to Top.

- Visual impact: 7/10.
- MBBS usefulness: 9/10.

Use Explore Universities, View Student Journeys, Compare Countries and Book Counselling. Emotional copy must be paired with trust information.

## 8. Legal and trust pages

Cookies explains eight areas including necessary cookies, similar technologies, consent management, third parties, duration and updates.

Privacy covers contact data, vehicle/enquiry data, uploaded files, technical data, lawful bases, sharing, international transfer, retention, marketing, children and user rights.

Terms cover website information, enquiries, quotations, modifications, acceptable use, user material, intellectual property, third-party websites, availability, responsibility, privacy and governing law.

For MBBS, the equivalent trust layer is critical because students may submit passports, marksheets, financial documents and personal information. Use clear consent, secure uploads, transparent retention, no guaranteed-admission claims and an explanation that final decisions belong to universities/regulators.

## 10. Technical and accessibility observations

The homepage exposed about 61 image elements and no HTML video elements in the inspected DOM. The cinematic effect therefore depends heavily on large assets and scroll/state transitions. Detail pages are image-heavy and sometimes provide video controls.

Risks for an MBBS build:

- Large hero media may delay the first useful message.
- Dark low-contrast type makes fees and eligibility difficult to scan.
- Scroll-only storytelling can exclude keyboard, reduced-motion and mobile users.
- Hover-only content fails on touch screens.
- Entry gates reduce conversion.
- Autoplay can distract students and parents.
- Respect the prefers-reduced-motion preference.
- Animated counters need static text equivalents.
- Images need meaningful alt text.
- Forms need visible labels, validation and keyboard access.
- Comparisons must not depend on visual position alone.

## 11. Recommended MBBS motion system

Use:

- 150–250 ms button/card transitions.
- 300–600 ms fade/slide reveals.
- One scroll-linked process section.
- Small-range image parallax.
- Counters only for verifiable numbers.
- Manual testimonial controls.
- Progress indicators for multi-step counselling.

Avoid:

- Forced entry gates.
- Blocking preload sequences.
- Constant particles.
- Excessive 3D objects.
- Rapid route transitions.
- Auto-advancing testimonials without pause.
- Motion that changes the order of fee/eligibility information.
- Effects that make medical education promises feel exaggerated.

## 12. MBBS implementation blueprint

Homepage:

1. Hero with student/campus image, value proposition and two CTAs.
2. Trust bar with verified support areas.
3. Three-step counselling process.
4. Country/university explorer.
5. Services.
6. Student stories.
7. Fee/eligibility explainer.
8. FAQ.
9. Contact and WhatsApp CTA.

Detail pages should cover countries, universities and student journeys. Each page should show facts first, narrative second and CTA third.

### Final recommendation

Forge’s strongest transferable idea is intentional progression. The best MBBS animation is the three-stage scroll narrative paired with a persistent counselling CTA. The least useful elements are the forced entry gate, extreme cinematic pacing and decorative climax. Build the MBBS site as a calm, credible version of Forge’s structure: premium enough to feel trustworthy, but clear enough that a parent can find eligibility, cost and next steps within seconds.
