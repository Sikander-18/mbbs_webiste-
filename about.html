Our Story

Why We Started This Consultancy

Dr. Nishu Yadav was in the same position as thousands of students today — NEET-qualified, determined to become a doctor, and navigating the confusing landscape of MBBS abroad alone. Most consultancies he encountered had no medical background. Advice was commission-driven and nobody prepared him for FMGE realities.

He completed his medical education across Ukraine and Kazakhstan, cleared FMGE on his first attempt with a strong score, and returned to India determined to raise the standard of admissions guidance. He saw too many students struggle — not because universities lacked quality, but because students received misleading advice at the time of admission.

He joined with Dr. Lokesh Attri and Dr. Bindu Tyagi — each with first-hand overseas medical education and first-attempt FMGE qualification — to found Stellar with a clear principle: medical admissions guidance must come from practitioners who have lived the curriculum, cleared regulatory licensing exams, and understand clinical training realities.

Founded in 2024, Stellar combines over a decade of cumulative academic and clinical experience across international medical faculties, offering objective evaluation of university faculties, clinical rotations, and regulatory compliance.
