# Peryton Film — Full Site, Motion and Animation Audit

Reference: https://www.peryton-film.com/  
Audit date: 8 September 2026  
Scope: all same-origin routes discovered from the primary navigation, footer, homepage cards, service links, job links, case-study links and knowledge hub. External Gumlet videos, Calendly, social networks, email, phone links and the implementation studio are treated as external assets, not additional site pages.

## 1. Executive summary

Peryton Film is a highly animated video-marketing website built around the idea of HYPE. Its visual system is dark green, gold and editorial. The homepage combines oversized kinetic type, a winged deer/3D hero object, star particles, scroll-linked service storytelling, social-media mockups, a draggable project wall, blur-to-focus typography, a large globe/planet sequence, testimonial videos, floating crew imagery, FAQ and a knowledge section.

Unlike Forge Automotive, which uses animation to create a premium atelier feeling, Peryton uses animation as part of the product story: it sells strategic video, social advertising and campaign performance by making the website itself feel like a moving campaign.

The strongest transferable lessons for an MBBS consultancy are:

- Keep one memorable visual idea across the site.
- Explain a service through a sequence rather than a static feature list.
- Use real proof and measurable outcomes.
- Create separate pages for services, case studies and questions.
- Turn testimonials into evidence, not decoration.
- Reuse one production asset across multiple channels.

The least transferable elements are constant particles, extreme 3D, draggable walls and very long animation sequences. They are visually impressive but would distract from eligibility, fees and admission accuracy.

## 3. Global design and motion language

### Visual identity

- Deep green background.
- Gold and warm neutral accents.
- Large uppercase display type.
- Gold PERYTON FILM branding.
- Star/particle field.
- Black or dark video frames.
- AI-generated symbolic imagery on the homepage.
- Strong contrast between still type and moving media.

### Global motion patterns observed

1. Persistent particles create depth behind content.
2. Hero typography and 3D object change as the visitor scrolls.
3. Phone/social-ad mockups move through different content states.
4. Service text appears beside media as the story progresses.
5. Project cards form a large interactive wall with a DRAG affordance.
6. Text moves from blur to focus to control emphasis.
7. A large gold planet/globe grows into the viewport.
8. Testimonial video cards use slider/blur transitions.
9. Floating team cutouts and AI imagery create a playful brand universe.
10. FAQ and article content provide a calmer information phase after the visual sequences.

For MBBS, use only the patterns that clarify a journey or prove trust. Decorative motion should be limited.

## 4. Homepage audit

### Hero: “Mit Videomarketing vom Geheimtipp zum HYPE”

The hero promises to move a brand from hidden potential to hype. The supporting line describes strategic films and social ads from Gießen to Frankfurt, for brands that want to be seen and bought.

Observed composition:

- Deep green field.
- Gold PERYTON FILM mark.
- Oversized HYPE typography.
- Gold 3D winged deer.
- Star particles.
- Antrieb zünden CTA leading to Calendly or Kontakt.

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **10/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **7/10**

**MBBS adaptation**

Use one ownable concept, for example “From confused applicant to confident doctor.” A 3D medical object is unnecessary; a refined animated compass, route line or university map is enough. The message must state the concrete outcome: shortlist, application and arrival support.

### HYPE service story

The homepage introduces the Peryton HYPE through stages labelled:

- Videomarketing.
- Infrastruktur.
- Weitergedacht.
- Verankerung.

A social/leadboard-style media sequence shows campaign information and simulated lead activity.

**Motion**

- Scroll-linked text and media.
- Phone/social UI changes state.
- Content is revealed as the visitor moves through the process.

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **9/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **9/10**

**MBBS version**

Use a four-stage student journey:

1. Discover — understand countries and realistic options.
2. Assess — evaluate NEET, academics and budget.
3. Apply — manage documents and applications.
4. Arrive — support visa, travel and onboarding.

Use a phone-like checklist or application timeline only if it is genuinely useful. Avoid fake leadboard data.

### Project wall: “Über 400 Projekte”

The homepage presents a broad portfolio sorted or labelled by industries:

- Industrie.
- Software and IT.
- Gastronomie.
- Event and Kultur.
- Handel and Einzelhandel.
- Bank and Finanzen.
- Gesundheit and Pflege.
- Handwerk.
- Vereine.
- Lebensmittel.
- Politik.
- Bildung and Beratung.

The page exposes a DRAG affordance and a large collection of media cards.

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **9/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **8/10**.

**MBBS use**

Create an interactive student/university wall only if there are enough genuine stories. Useful filters could be:

- Country.
- Student profile.
- Budget.
- Intake.
- Course stage.

A smaller grid with clear filters is safer than a dramatic draggable wall. Keyboard controls, visible labels and a non-drag fallback are essential.

### Strategy statement

The homepage moves through statements equivalent to:

- Infinite possibilities.
- We find out what matters and choose the right route.
- Strong ideas emerge.
- We show them to the right people.
- That creates HYPE.

The visual treatment includes blur-to-focus text and a man in a white shirt.

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **9/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **9/10**

**MBBS adaptation**

Use the same structure:

- Many countries exist.
- We find out what matters for your profile.
- We create a realistic shortlist.
- We explain the next step to you and your family.
- Confidence replaces confusion.

This is a strong use of animation because each visual state corresponds to a decision.

### Gold planet/globe

A large gold planet grows into view with a figure on top. It acts as a visual metaphor for scale, ambition and direction.

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **9/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **6/10**

Use a world map or globe only if it helps students compare countries. A static/interactive map with real filters is more useful than a decorative planet.

### Video testimonials

The homepage includes multiple embedded Gumlet video testimonials from clients in retail, food, education, finance, events and other sectors.

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **8/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **10/10**

This is one of the strongest patterns for MBBS. Use student and parent video testimonials with:

- Name or approved first name.
- Country/university.
- Student profile.
- Service received.
- Clear consent.
- Captions and transcript.
- Manual play.

### Floating crew section

The homepage presents AI-generated or stylised imagery and the message “Die richtige Crew für große Missionen,” with a link to About.

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **8/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **6/10**.

Use real counsellor portraits and role labels instead. A consultancy needs human credibility more than surreal floating objects.

### FAQ and knowledge section

The homepage ends with FAQ and an article preview.

The FAQ topics include:

- What is a film with a story?
- Why does it work?
- Who needs actors?
- What is the Peryton concept?
- What is a retainer?
- Why use a campaign?
- What makes Peryton different?
- How does it fit an existing strategy?

**Ratings**

- Visual impact: **5/10**
- MBBS usefulness: **10/10**

For MBBS, answer eligibility, country recognition, fees, safety, visa, documents, timelines, refunds, accommodation, support after arrival and what the consultancy can and cannot guarantee.

## 8. Case-study audit

Each case-study page follows a persuasive structure:

1. Client, industry, location and duration.
2. Ausgangslage / starting situation.
3. Das haben wir gemacht / what was done.
4. Das ist das Ergebnis / outcome.
5. Embedded video or testimonial.

This is an excellent structure for MBBS proof.

### Sparkasse Marburg-Biedenkopf

A regional advisory campaign used real advisers and short scenes to make banking services understandable. It ran across cinema, social and out-of-home and led to additional campaigns.

**MBBS lesson:** show real counsellors or students explaining one specific decision.

### REWE Mitte Einkaufsbus

A rural shopping-bus film used real residents and the real driver to make a service emotionally credible. It developed into long-term work.

**MBBS lesson:** real students and parents are more trustworthy than generic stock imagery.

### Gießener Kultursommer 2026

A faster campaign used short formats, humour, recognisable faces, local places and graphic animation to build recall and regional connection.

**MBBS lesson:** repeated faces and a consistent format can make an educational brand familiar. Use graphic animation for deadlines or document steps.

### Licher Privatbrauerei

Recurring content pillars used real company faces and a consistent brand style. The first post reportedly reached over 1,100 likes and led to more productions.

**MBBS lesson:** create recurring series, not one disconnected campaign.

### Sport- und Modehaus Kaps

A running campaign connected social ads with a landing page, registration and an in-store QR-code benefit. It generated over 1,100 regional registrations.

**MBBS lesson:** connect an ad to one concrete next step, such as an eligibility form or counselling slot.

### CONTI Sanitärarmaturen

A complex B2B water-management product was represented by the metaphor of a conductor controlling an orchestra. The film’s visual language influenced a later trade-show stand.

**MBBS lesson:** explain a complex admission process through one simple metaphor, but do not let metaphor replace factual details.

### SES Germany

A 30-year anniversary film used multiple story strands, humour, a family and a resolution to explain company history and services.

**MBBS lesson:** a student’s “before, decision, support, after” story can explain the service naturally.

### GUUL Translations

A performance campaign used different creatives for different audiences and led visitors toward a simple app sign-up. The page reports about 2,000 enquiries and additional revenue.

**MBBS lesson:** segment by student profile, country and budget rather than showing one generic message.

### WERTGESCHÄTZT

A sensitive subject was communicated with warmth and quality. The same ads generated daily leads over an extended period.

**MBBS lesson:** discuss anxiety, safety and family concerns with empathy; do not use aggressive urgency.

### Friedberg Open Air 2026

The campaign used local references, humour, artist-specific creatives and seasonal purchase moments.

**MBBS lesson:** build content around real student decision moments: exam results, counselling season, application deadlines and visa milestones.

### Lokschuppen Marburg

A venue launch used a premium but explosive visual language to present multiple areas under one brand.

**MBBS lesson:** unify counselling, admissions and arrival support under one recognizable visual system.

### ZVEH Light + Building

Real employees were used at a trade event to create a professional, approachable film that continued into regular work.

**MBBS lesson:** show real advisors and actual working processes, not only polished campus photos.

### Gießener Kultursommer 2025

Local landmarks, real faces and AI hooks were used for attention. The page reports around 80,000 tickets and strong ROAS on many ads.

**MBBS lesson:** localise content for Indian students and parents, but clearly distinguish real facts from creative illustration.

### Möbelstadt Sommerlad

A recruiting campaign used a detective story with actors instead of listing benefits. It reportedly generated around 90 qualified applications and several hires.

**MBBS lesson:** a short story can make a complicated decision memorable, but all claims about university admission must remain accurate.

## 10. Technical, accessibility and performance observations

The homepage exposed approximately 120 images and 61 video elements. Many testimonial/project media items come from Gumlet or other remote media delivery. The pages therefore have high asset weight and a meaningful dependency on video loading.

Potential issues:

- Large video files can delay page interaction.
- Several media areas exposed an “Unable to play media” state in the accessibility tree during inspection.
- A very long scroll narrative can make the main CTA hard to reach.
- Particle fields and 3D effects may consume mobile GPU resources.
- Drag interactions need keyboard and touch alternatives.
- Blur transitions can reduce readability and cause motion discomfort.
- Auto-playing media needs pause, mute and caption controls.
- Video testimonials need transcript text.
- AI-generated visuals need clear disclosure where relevant.
- Animated counters need stable, visible values.
- Every service and case study needs a semantic heading structure.
- Reduced-motion users need a static layout equivalent.

## 11. MBBS-specific recommendation matrix

### Best patterns to adopt

1. Three- or four-stage scroll process for counselling.
2. Case-study template with situation, work and outcome.
3. Student/parent video testimonials.
4. FAQ and knowledge hub.
5. Service detail pages with clear inclusions.
6. Reusable content system for social and SEO.
7. Booking CTA repeated at decision points.
8. Verified metrics shown as static text as well as animation.

### Patterns to adapt carefully

1. World map: make it functional, not decorative.
2. Animated counters: only use verified data.
3. Phone UI: show a real application checklist, not fake leads.
4. Portfolio wall: use filters and a normal grid fallback.
5. Large hero type: preserve readability and search intent.

### Patterns to avoid

1. Constant star particles.
2. Heavy 3D objects.
3. Unbounded draggable layouts.
4. Autoplay testimonial/video walls.
5. Blur that hides text.
6. Fake leadboard or simulated application data.
7. Exaggerated urgency.
8. Visuals that imply guaranteed admissions.

## Final recommendation

Peryton’s most valuable lesson is that a business website can feel like a campaign while still using a deep information architecture underneath. For MBBS, adopt the strategic storytelling, testimonials, case studies, FAQs and recurring content. Use animation to clarify the decision path—Profile, Shortlist, Apply, Arrive—not to create spectacle for its own sake.

The best Peryton-inspired MBBS experience would be memorable, warm and premium, but it would always leave eligibility, cost, recognition and the next counselling step visible and understandable.
