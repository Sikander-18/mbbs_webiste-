## FAQs

### Q: Which Kazakhstan university has the best FMGE rate?

Al-Farabi Kazakh National University leads at ~51% FMGE pass rate. Astana Medical University is second at ~35.9%. KazNMU (Asfendiyarov) follows at ~27%. For budget options, Semey and Karaganda are solid at 21–25%.

### Q: Why only 5 years in Kazakhstan?

Kazakhstan follows a 5-year MBBS program (similar to European countries). It is fully recognized by NMC. This saves you 1 full year and ₹3–6L in costs compared to Russia.

### Q: Is Kazakhstan cheaper than Russia?

Yes. Total cost in Kazakhstan: ₹15–30L. Russia: ₹24–45L. Kazakhstan is 15–40% cheaper depending on universities compared.

### Q: Is MBBS from Kazakhstan valid in India?

Yes, from NMC-approved, WDOMS-listed universities, after clearing FMGE/NExT. All our recommended Kazakhstan universities meet this.

### Q: Almaty or Astana — which city is better?

Almaty is generally preferred — milder climate, larger Indian community, and Al-Farabi (top university) is located there. Astana has Astana Medical University with excellent FMGE results but harsher winters.
