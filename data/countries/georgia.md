## FAQs

### Q: Is Georgia safe for Indian students?

Yes, Georgia is considered one of the safest countries for international students in the region.

### Q: Is MBBS from Georgia NMC approved?

Yes, multiple Georgian universities are on the NMC approved list. We only work with compliant universities.

### Q: What language is teaching in?

All MBBS programs for international students are taught fully in English.

### Q: Can I easily visit India from Georgia?

Yes, direct and connecting flights available. Travel time is approximately 6–8 hours.

### Q: What is the FMGE pass rate from Georgia?

Approximately 50–55%, one of the higher rates among popular MBBS abroad destinations.
