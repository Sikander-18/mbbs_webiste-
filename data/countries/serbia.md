## FAQs

### Q: What programs are available in Serbia?

Bachelor's and Master's programs at Alfa BK University (various fields), and Medicine at University of Kragujevac. Both are in Belgrade/Kragujevac — accessible European cities.

### Q: What is the total cost of studying in Serbia?

Bachelor's (girls): ~€1,000/year tuition. Bachelor's (boys): ~€1,950/year. Masters: €1,200–€2,100/year. Plus ~€150/month accommodation and €150/month food. One-time company charge: €1,000.

### Q: Can I work while studying in Serbia?

Yes! Stellar Science Hub & Educonsultancy helps students find part-time jobs while studying, and assists with placement in UAE and Germany after graduation.

### Q: Is the degree from Serbia recognised internationally?

Yes. Serbian university degrees are recognised across Europe. Stellar Science Hub handles 12th marksheet equivalence and recognition as part of the package.

### Q: How long is the visa processing?

Typically 2–4 weeks. Stellar Science Hub & Educonsultancy handles all visa documentation as part of the €500 visa processing service.
