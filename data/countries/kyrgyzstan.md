## FAQs

### Q: Is Kyrgyzstan a good option for low-budget students?

Yes, if you are budget-constrained and committed to consistent FMGE preparation. The low fees make it accessible, but FMGE pass rates require focused effort.

### Q: What is the FMGE pass rate from Kyrgyzstan?

Approximately 30–40%. Lower than other destinations. However, students who prepare consistently from Year 1 have much better outcomes.

### Q: Are there good hospitals for clinical training?

Clinical training quality varies by university. We only recommend universities with good hospital tie-ups.

### Q: Is Kyrgyzstan NMC approved?

Yes, our partner universities in Kyrgyzstan are NMC-approved and WDOMS-listed.

### Q: How long is the visa process?

Typically 2–3 weeks. We assist with all visa documentation to ensure a smooth process.
