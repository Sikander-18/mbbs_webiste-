## FAQs

### Q: Is Philippines good for USMLE preparation?

Yes, Philippines is one of the best countries for students targeting USMLE. The US-style curriculum and English-medium education provide strong preparation.

### Q: Is the climate in Philippines suitable?

Philippines is tropical and warm year-round. Perfect for students from warmer Indian states. Campuses and dorms are air-conditioned.

### Q: Is English widely spoken?

Yes, English is an official language in Philippines. There is zero language barrier.

### Q: What is the FMGE pass rate from Philippines?

Approximately 50–60%. The US-style curriculum aligns well with FMGE patterns.

### Q: Is Philippines NMC approved?

Yes, our partner universities in Philippines are NMC-approved and WDOMS-listed.
