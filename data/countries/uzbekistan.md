## FAQs

### Q: Is Uzbekistan safe for Indian students?

Yes, Uzbekistan is one of the safest destinations in Central Asia. Very low crime rates and welcoming to foreigners.

### Q: Is the weather in Uzbekistan suitable for Indians?

Yes! The climate is similar to north India — hot summers and mild winters. Much more comfortable than Russia or Kazakhstan.

### Q: Are Uzbekistan universities NMC approved?

Yes, we only work with NMC-approved, WDOMS-listed universities in Uzbekistan.

### Q: What is the total cost of MBBS in Uzbekistan?

Total cost over 5–6 years is approximately ₹18–35L, making it one of the most affordable options.

### Q: How is the food situation for vegetarians?

Vegetarian options are available. Indian mess and restaurants are present in cities with large Indian student communities.
