## 13. Issues and content-quality observations

1. Legal pages are broken: `/privacy` and `/disclaimer` lead to the generic 404 page.
2. `/book` contains a visible technical setup note about EmailJS; this should be removed or replaced by a user-facing error-free form before launch.
3. The homepage says “6 NMC-Approved Countries” in one section while the country index, footer, and cards show 7 countries. This count should be made consistent.
4. The homepage has mixed figures: the hero says 7 countries and 4 doctor counselors, while a later CTA says 3 doctors. The team/about sections display 3 named doctors but the about-page statistic says 4 doctors on the team.
5. Some homepage testimonials mention Nepal and universities not represented in the current seven-country index, which may confuse users.
6. Serbia is presented alongside MBBS destinations, but its own page qualifies that some listed programs are bachelor’s/master’s programs and that medicine/FMGE eligibility should be separately verified.
7. FMGE rates, NMC/WDOMS status, university fees, and claims such as “Top 200 globally” are high-stakes and time-sensitive; they should be supported by dated sources and independently verified before reuse.
8. The site has a disclaimer in footer copy saying information is for guidance only, but the linked disclaimer route is not available.

## 14. Useful content to adapt for your own MBBS consultancy website

- Hero: doctor-led counseling + a clear promise of no hidden charges.
- Trust bar: NMC/WDOMS verification, doctor counselors, FMGE planning, and student support.
- Country comparison table with total cost, duration, language, internship, FMGE range, and climate.
- University directory with fee, location, established year, accreditation status, and transparent limitations.
- Admission process timeline from NEET to visa and arrival.
- Eligibility calculator or score-to-destination guide.
- Document checklist download.
- FAQ groups for eligibility, fees, visa, safety, life abroad, FMGE/NExT, and post-MBBS options.
- Doctor profile pages showing actual study destination and relevant counseling expertise.
- Student stories with clear context and consent.
- Strong but responsible disclaimer and dated verification of all regulatory/fee data.
