## 2. Core Value Proposition & Brand Angles (To Use on Your Website)

- **The Hook:** *"For the Doctors. By the Doctors."*
- **The Core Problem:** Most overseas medical consultancies are commercial sales operations staffed by sales agents who have never stepped inside a foreign medical university.
- **The Solution:** Every single counselor is a foreign medical graduate (doctor) who has lived, studied, passed medical licensing exams, and practiced.
- **Key Trust Factors:**
  1. 100% NMC (National Medical Commission) and WDOMS compliant universities only.
  2. Zero capitation fee & direct university fee payment.
  3. Integrated FMGE/NExT coaching roadmap from Day 1 (before boarding flight).
  4. Complete on-ground support (airport pickup, hostel allocation, local SIM, bank account, police registration).
