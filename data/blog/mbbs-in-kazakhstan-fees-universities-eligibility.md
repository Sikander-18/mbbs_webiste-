Kazakhstan is Central Asia's largest country and has been rapidly developing its higher education infrastructure. For Indian students, it offers:

5-year MBBS program (saving 1 year vs Russia/Georgia) Affordable fees (₹20–40L total) NMC-approved universities Growing Indian student community Why Kazakhstan Instead of Russia?

The honest answer: Kazakhstan is cheaper than Russia for comparable quality, and the 5-year program is a genuine advantage. The FMGE pass rates are slightly lower (~35–45%) compared to Russia, but with focused preparation the gap narrows.
