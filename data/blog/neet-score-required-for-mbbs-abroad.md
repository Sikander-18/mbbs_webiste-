## Key Points & Lists

- More prestigious universities require higher scores — not officially, but competitive Indian students choose better universities
- Your score affects your options — some universities look at academic performance holistically
- NMC may scrutinize very low scores — though currently there's no official score minimum

Many students think you need a high NEET score for MBBS abroad. The reality is more nuanced. Here's the honest country-wise breakdown.

Talk to a doctor who has studied MBBS abroad — free, no obligation.

Foreign medical universities do not have a minimum NEET score requirement beyond NEET qualification. The NMC only requires that you have passed NEET — there is no score minimum for admission abroad.

This is fundamentally different from Indian private colleges where NEET score determines cutoff.
